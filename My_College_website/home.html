About Our College

Government City College, Hyderabad is an autonomous public institution established in 1921,
affiliated with Osmania University and accredited with NAAC A++ grade. The college
offers over 50 UG and PG programs in science, arts, and commerce, with affordable fees
and strong placement support. Its campus features iconic heritage architecture and
is located near the Telangana High Court on the banks of the Musi River


History of city college

Government City College Hyderabad has a rich history that dates back to 1865, beginning as Madarsa
Dar-ul-Uloom founded by the sixth Nizam, Mahbub Ali Khan Bahadur. It was transformed over the decades,
eventually moving into its iconic Indo-Saracenic style building on the banks of the Musi river in 1921
under the patronage of Mir Osman Ali Khan, the last Nizam of Hyderabad. This building was designed by
the British architect Vincent Jerome Esch, marking one of Hyderabad’s prominent educational structures.
