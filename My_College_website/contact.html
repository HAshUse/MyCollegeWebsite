Government City College detailes

Government City College, Hyderabad, is a prestigious autonomous public college established in 1921,
affiliated with Osmania University. It offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs across Science,
Commerce, and Arts disciplines. The college holds a high accreditation with a NAAC "A++" grade and is
recognized by UGC and AICTE. Located on High Court Road, Ghansi Bazaar, Hyderabad, it has a reputation
for quality education and offers courses such as B.Sc, B.Com, BA, M.Sc, MA, and M.Com with admissions
primarily merit-based via the Telangana DOST portal for UG and TS CPGET for PG courses. The college
also has good infrastructure facilities like hostels, canteen, library, labs, and sports complex
